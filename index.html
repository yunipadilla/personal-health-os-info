Personal Health OS

Personal Health OS is a private, local-first desktop application for personal health and performance tracking.

The application combines manually entered health and wellness information with data from authorized health integrations, including Oura, to help the user review personal trends and understand changes over time.

Personal Health OS is currently intended for personal development and testing. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Oura data is accessed only after explicit user authorization and is used to provide functionality requested by the user.
