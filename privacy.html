Personal Health OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Information We Process

Personal Health OS may process information intentionally entered by the user, including nutrition information, journal entries, subjective wellbeing ratings, and other personal health information.

If the user connects an Oura account, Personal Health OS may access only the categories of Oura data authorized by the user.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide personal health tracking, historical analysis, personalized insights, and related functionality requested by the user.

Storage

Personal Health OS is designed as a local-first desktop application. Health records are primarily stored locally on the user's computer.

Authentication credentials and tokens are protected separately using operating-system security mechanisms where supported.

Artificial Intelligence

Authorized health information may be processed by an artificial intelligence service when necessary to provide an analysis or response requested by the user and when permitted by applicable agreements and user consent.

Oura data will not be used to train, fine-tune, develop, or improve an artificial intelligence or machine-learning model.

Sharing

Personal Health OS does not sell personal health information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user and permitted by applicable agreements and user consent.

Retention and Deletion

The user controls locally stored Personal Health OS information. Users may disconnect supported integrations and may delete locally stored information.

Authorization and Consent

Oura information is accessed only after explicit OAuth authorization. Users may revoke authorization by disconnecting the integration or through available Oura account controls.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect locally stored information and authentication credentials.

Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be sent to the contact email associated with the Personal Health OS Oura developer application.
